Advertising & Partnerships

Citizen Energy News covers the citizen-led energy transition, Sunlight Income Villages, local renewables and the social and solidarity economy in Korean, Japanese, Chinese and English.

Readers: local government and public agency energy officers, cooperatives and social economy groups, renewable energy companies, researchers, and citizen-energy practitioners abroad.

Formats: (1) banners on the front page and below articles (2) sponsored articles clearly labelled "Advertisement" (3) event and call-for-proposal notices (4) simultaneous publication in four languages.

Principle: advertising and news are clearly separated; news is edited independently of advertisers.

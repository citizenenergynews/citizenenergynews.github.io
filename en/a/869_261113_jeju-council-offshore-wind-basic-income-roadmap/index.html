Jeju council presses on 5 GW offshore wind in pledge plan, demands basic income roadmap

AI Reporter Kim · Checked by the editorAI translation2026.11.13

Committee chair Kim Ki-hwan calls for a basic income roadmap / Province moves to put 5 GW into the 12th Basic Plan for Electricity Supply and Demand

The Jeju Provincial Council questioned the offshore wind target and the energy basic income in the pledge implementation plan during the governor-questioning session at the plenary of its 456th second regular session on Nov. 13. Kim Ki-hwan, chair of the future economy and industry committee, said Governor Wi Seong-gon's campaign pledges of 100 trillion won in investment, 10 GW of offshore wind and an annual 1 trillion won basic income were missing from the plan finalised in October. Only 5 GW of offshore wind remains, he said, and the plan goes no further than proposing its inclusion in the national 12th Basic Plan for Electricity Supply and Demand.

Kim said Jeju's internal grid can absorb at most 1 GW more and that a link to the mainland grid is uncertain, and asked whether the pledges had been scrapped or postponed. He also said that a scheme in which some residents invest and receive dividends differs from a development profit sharing fund (a fund built from the profit-sharing payments of wind developers) shared by all residents. He asked whether fishers and residents of maritime border areas sit on the 17-member offshore wind support team, and, if a basic income is not feasible now, asked for a roadmap with its timing, conditions and per-person payment.

This paper could not confirm Governor Wi's reply at the plenary. Earlier, at a press conference on Oct. 7 marking 100 days of the ninth elected administration, the province said it would put a 5 GW offshore wind development plan for the Jeju region into the 12th Basic Plan.

Reading the News The text of the Oct. 7 press conference also says the Jeju Wind Power Ordinance was fully revised to lay a base for wider resident participation built on sharing development profits. On Nov. 12, this paper reported in [Jeju offshore wind 5 GW governor questioning](897) on questions about the transmission route for power from the Chuja offshore wind project. The governor replied that a direct link to Haenam or Jindo would be about 50 km, and that the government is negative about a route that detours 150 km.

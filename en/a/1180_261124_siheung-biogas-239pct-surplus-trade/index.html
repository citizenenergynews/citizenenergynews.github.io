Siheung hits 239% of biogas target, earns 107 million won from surplus

AI Reporter Kim · Checked by the editorAI translation2026.11.24

It treated 745 tonnes a day of sewage sludge and other waste to produce 8.86 million Nm³ in 2025. The remaining 3.58 million Nm³ surplus will be traded directly with other public producers by January 20, 2027.

Siheung in Gyeonggi Province said on November 24 that it had reached 239% of its 2025 biogas production target. It also earned about 107 million won in non-tax revenue by selling part of its surplus production record to other local governments. The 2025 target was 3.7 million Nm³, and actual production was 8.86 million Nm³ (Nm³ is gas volume converted to standard conditions). Of the 5.16 million Nm³ surplus, 1.58 million Nm³ was traded through centralised trading, in which a system matches buyers and sellers.

The biogas is produced by the Clean Energy Center inside the city's Water Environment Center, which treats 745 tonnes a day of organic waste such as sewage sludge, livestock manure and food waste. The remaining 3.58 million Nm³ of surplus will be sold by January 20, 2027 through voluntary trading, negotiated directly with public mandatory producers such as other local governments. Sewage biogas has wide uses: the Cheongju Public Sewage Treatment Plant used it to produce 26,801kg of hydrogen over four months from May to August.

The trade is based on the "biogas production target system" under the Act on the Promotion of Production and Use of Biogas Using Organic Waste Resources. The system has applied to public mandatory producers since 2025 and to private mandatory producers since 2026. The production target rate is a share of the maximum amount of biogas that can be produced from organic waste. The plan the Ministry of Environment set out when it pre-announced the subordinate regulations in 2023 was to start the public rate at 50%, keep it for the first five years and raise it to 80% from 2045. Public mandatory producers that exceed their targets can transfer the surplus to other public mandatory producers, while those that fall short pay a penalty surcharge.

On November 11, the Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment pre-announced a draft notice setting the 2027 production target rates at 50% for public and 10% for private producers. Opinions can be submitted to the minister by post, email or fax until December 1.

Citizen Energy View

Siheung turned sewage sludge and food waste, once seen only as something to dispose of, into more than twice its target of gas, and earned over 100 million won from its surplus record. But the more local governments buy records instead, the later their own waste-to-energy facilities may be built. Waste-based energy is a public asset that comes from residents' own waste, so trading revenue should also go back into improving facilities and the local energy transition. Citizens and local councils should demand disclosure of how the remaining 3.58 million Nm³ is traded and how the revenue is used.

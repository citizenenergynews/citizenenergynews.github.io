Saemangeum RE100 solar 30MW opens third call for developers

AI Reporter Kim · Written and source-checked by AIAI translation2026.11.23

Saemangeum Development Corp. will take proposals on November 30 only. It may hold up to 29.9% of the project company, and power will be sold to tenant firms through direct PPAs.

Saemangeum Development Corp. is recruiting, for the third time, a developer for a 30MW land-based solar project that will supply renewable power to companies in the Saemangeum Smart Green National Pilot Industrial Complex. Proposals will be accepted only in person from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. on November 30 at the seminar room on the first floor of the corporation's office in Gunsan. On November 13 the corporation announced that the previous re-tender had failed because only one consortium submitted a proposal, and it issued a new notice on the 19th.

The site is about 448,390㎡ (about 135,000 pyeong) of public water surface near Osikdo-dong, Gunsan, and the plant will connect to the grid at Korea Electric Power Corporation's Naecho substation. The operating period is 20 years from the start of commercial operation, and bidders propose their own start date. If the developer misses that date through its own fault, it must pay liquidated damages of 0.05% of the total investment per day, capped at 10% of the total investment.

The developer must set up a special purpose company (SPC) within 30 days of signing the agreement, and the corporation may invest up to 29.9% of its shares. The largest shareholder may not hold more than 50%, and the developer must keep 20% of the total investment as equity until operation ends. A consortium must include a power supplier, a firm with a project financing record of at least 40 billion won within the past 10 years, and a contractor that has completed a solar plant of 3MW or more.

Power must first be sold to tenant or prospective tenant companies in the complex under direct power purchase agreements, at a price within 105% of the supply price. If no company in the complex buys, the developer may, with the corporation's approval, sign one-year contracts with companies outside the complex. In the 100-point evaluation, total investment per MW and total revenue are worth 20 points each, and the share of local contractors in construction is worth 7 points. Elsewhere in Saemangeum, Gunsan Citizen Power Co., wholly owned by Gunsan City, has run a 100MW land-based solar plant since December 2021. Written questions are accepted until 2 p.m. on the 23rd, and proposals are scheduled to be evaluated on December 9.

Citizen Energy View

The project will run 30MW on 135,000 pyeong of public water surface for 20 years, yet not one item in the 100-point evaluation table looks at participation by residents or citizens. Gunsan Citizen Power's land-based solar plant in the same Saemangeum area set aside 5.07 billion won, 4% of the project cost, for nearby residents and has paid it out every six months. Now that even the re-tender has failed is the moment to put a share for local residents and citizen investors inside the corporation's 29.9% stake. Saemangeum Development Corp. and Gunsan City should add points for resident participation to the next call, and citizens of Jeonbuk should take that demand to the corporation directly.

Seoul council asks Seoul Energy Corp. to finish surveys of 40 public car parks

AI Reporter Kim · Checked by the editorAI translation2026.11.13

The utility moved from a 64.9 billion won loss in 2023 to profit, but customer satisfaction fell to 79.4 in 2025

Wi Seong-chan of the Seoul Metropolitan Council's environment and water resources committee (People Power Party, proportional seat) called for more solar on public car parks at an audit of Seoul Energy Corp. on Nov. 10. Wi asked the company to finish, without delay, the site-survey study now under way at 40 public car parks and to widen cooperation with large private car parks.

According to Korea Energy Agency guidance, the installation duty covers car parks with 1,000 square metres or more of parking-bay area that are run by the state, local governments, public institutions and similar bodies. The required capacity is at least 1 kW per 10 square metres of effective area (effective area is the parking-bay area minus any area where installing equipment is unreasonable). Wi pointed to a structure in which the company's results swing sharply with LNG prices; the company went from a 64.9 billion won loss in 2023 to a profit in 2025. First-half net profit in 2026 was 28.1 billion won.

Customer satisfaction fell from 82.9 in 2024 to 79.4 in 2025, and was more than five points below the average for comparable public corporations for a third straight year (based on data the company submitted).

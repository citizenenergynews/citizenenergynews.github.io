Two Suwon energy cooperatives to build a garden in Yuljeon-dong on Nov. 25, with KRW 13 million for urban forest

AI Reporter Kim · Written and source-checked by AIAI translation2026.11.12

Members' fortnight runs Nov. 12–25; briefing on Suwon Haetbit Power Co. announced for Nov. 16 at Suwon City Council

The Suwon Citizen Solar Social Cooperative and the Suwon Energy Cooperative are running a members' fortnight from Nov. 12 to 25 and have announced two events. On Nov. 2 they said they would hold a founding briefing for Suwon Haetbit Power Co. from 2 p.m. to 5 p.m. on Nov. 16 in the first-floor multipurpose hall of Suwon City Council. Any Suwon resident can sign up through a Google form, and the briefing covers why the company is being set up, its future projects and how citizens can take part. The company is being formed by the city and local energy cooperatives to add solar plants.

Urban forest donation and tree planting The two cooperatives said on Nov. 11 that they will give KRW 13 million to the city's "10 Million Trees Urban Forest" project, tied to solar power income. The money will pay for a garden on about 80㎡ of traffic-island land in Yuljeon-dong, Jangan District. Suwon Haetbit Power is a special purpose company (SPC, a company set up for a single business) targeted for founding in the first half of 2027. A tree-planting event for members will run from 10:30 a.m. to 1 p.m. on Nov. 25 next to Yuljeon Children's Park at 599 Yuljeon-dong.

528 solar income villages selected, third-round results due in late December

AI Reporter Kim · Checked by the editorAI translation2026.11.24

Minister of Climate, Energy and Environment Kim Sung-whan met about 60 people, including resident representatives, in Seoul on November 23. Villages selected on land, grid or funding conditions were set to present their cases.

The Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment said on November 23 that it would hold a meeting with residents of solar income villages from 2 p.m. that day at Korea Electric Power Corporation's Gyeongin Construction Headquarters in Jung-gu, Seoul. About 60 people, including the solar income village task force, energy-related agencies and village resident representatives, are expected to attend. It is a chance for Minister Kim Sung-whan to hear directly about the progress, suggestions and difficulties of villages selected in the first call.

The two-hour meeting was set up so that, after the task force's report on the program's progress, four villages each present their case for five minutes, followed by 67 minutes of suggestions and open discussion. Chojeon Village in Gimhae, South Gyeongsang, was selected as an "immediate start" village that can begin its project right away. Seobong-ri Village in Wanju County, North Jeolla, was selected on a land condition, Osaeng 2-ri Village in Eumseong County, North Chungcheong, on a grid condition, and Donpo 2-ri Village in Asan, South Chungcheong, on a funding condition. Chojeon Village (Jillye-myeon, Gimhae), cited as a leading case, obtained its power generation business license while preparing for the first call, and its solar facility is due to be completed at the end of November.

The ministry decided to bring suggestions from the meeting that require interministerial cooperation or institutional improvement to the "Renewable Energy Deployment Innovation Task Force (TF)" for discussion. This body is a government-wide council formed by ministries, public institutions and local governments (the targets of the First Basic Plan for Renewable Energy are 100GW by 2030 and a generation share of at least 30% by 2035). Minister Kim said, "Since each village's conditions and state of preparation differ, we must find answers in the voices from the field."

In December last year, the government reported the "plan to spread solar income villages nationwide" to the Cabinet, and in February this year it launched a government-wide task force and set up a joint public-private field support team. It selected 87 villages in the first call (March 31 to May 31) and 441 in the second (June 1 to July 31), 528 in total. The third call, which took applications from September 17 to October 30, will be evaluated and the selection results will be released in late December.

Citizen Energy View

528 villages have been selected, but three of the four villages slated to present that day carried land, grid or funding conditions. Grid connection is in the hands of KEPCO, funding in those of financial institutions and land in those of local governments, so residents cannot solve these alone. The progress on tasks brought to the task force, when and how they are resolved, should be shared village by village so residents can plan their project schedules. Residents of conditionally selected villages should lay out their blocked conditions in detail and keep informing the task force and local governments.

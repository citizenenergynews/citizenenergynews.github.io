None of Five Key Tasks for Korea's 100GW Renewables Goal Is Complete as Climate Solutions Releases Traffic Light Review

AI Reporter Kim · Checked by the editorAI translation2026.11.12

Deployment at 47.2GW, or 47.2% of the goal, with 0.36GW built of 36GW of permitted offshore wind

In the "Renewable Energy 100GW Traffic Light" that Climate Solutions released on November 12, none of the five policy tasks needed to reach the 2030 goal received a green light. Ending fossil fuel privileges and securing grid neutrality are red, while priority grid access, electricity market modernization and removing permitting barriers are yellow. Deployment as tallied by Climate Solutions is 47.2GW, or 47.2% of the target, and installed capacity was about 30GW in 2023.

Renewable and energy storage (ESS) operators cannot take part in the committees under the Korea Power Exchange that discuss electricity market rules. Even after 27 coal units (about 14GW) are converted to gas, the cost-based compensation system (which guarantees investment costs and profits) remains. As of December 2025, only 0.36GW (1%) of the 36GW of permitted offshore wind had been built, and no agrivoltaic zone designation has been confirmed.

In its first Basic Plan for Renewable Energy in May, the government set targets of 100GW of capacity by 2030 and a renewable share of at least 30% of generation by 2035.

Read the News Climate Solutions proposed an independent regulator and a separation of the grid business, and called for market, grid and permitting rules to be written into the 12th Basic Plan for Electricity Supply and Demand and Korea Electric Power Corporation's investment plan. The group said the traffic light assessments will be updated as policies change. The full issue brief and press release are available on its website (forourclimate.org).

Chungju Industrial Complex 1 to get 14MW of solar and ESS for 34.56 billion won

AI Reporter Kim · Written and source-checked by AIAI translation2026.11.22

Chungbuk Technopark leads the project, which also helps 20 tenant companies replace equipment by December 2029. National funds cover 20 billion won and private investors 11.559 billion won.

Chungju Industrial Complex 1 in North Chungcheong Province will get 14MW of solar power and a 2MWh energy storage system (ESS) by 2029. The provincial government said on 20 November that the complex was finally selected on 12 November in the Ministry of Trade and Industry's call for the "industrial complex energy self-sufficient infrastructure construction and operation project". The project is part of the 2026 Smart Green Industrial Complex promotion programme, and the province said nine smart green complexes were selected nationwide, with Chungju Complex 1 the one selected from the province.

The project runs from November 2026 to December 2029 with a total budget of 34.559 billion won. National funds provide 20 billion won, the province 900 million won and the city 2.1 billion won, while private participants put in the remaining 11.559 billion won. Besides solar and storage, it will build an integrated energy management system and help 20 tenant companies diagnose and replace equipment with high-efficiency models.

Chungbuk Technopark leads the project, joined by the province, Chungju City, YPP, Korea District Heating Corporation, Impix and Geumgang Electric Industry, among others. Korea Industrial Complex Corporation, Chungbuk Technopark and the participating companies set up a cooperation framework through a business agreement on 19 November.

Busan's Sinpyeong-Jangnim Industrial Complex (built in 1990), selected earlier for the ministry's industrial complex energy self-sufficiency infrastructure project, included a plan to share generation income. According to Busan City's release of 13 July, the complex will build factory-rooftop solar with 32 billion won, including 20 billion won of national funds. Eight organisations and companies, including Korea Southern Power, take part. Part of the income will be returned to tenant companies and workers as "sunlight income" and carbon vouchers, and reinvested in carbon-reduction facilities. The Chungbuk provincial release does not say how income from the Chungju complex will be shared. The province plans to build the renewable facilities and improve tenant companies' efficiency in stages through 2029.

Citizen Energy View

Of the 34.559 billion won budget for Chungju Complex 1, 23 billion won comes from national, provincial and city funds. Busan's Sinpyeong-Jangnim complex wrote into its plan that part of the income will go as far as workers, but Chungju's release has no such commitment yet. An industrial complex becomes a local power plant when income from electricity made on factory roofs is shared not only with equipment investors but also with tenant workers and nearby residents. Chungbuk Province and Chungju City should disclose their income-sharing principles before the operating stage begins.

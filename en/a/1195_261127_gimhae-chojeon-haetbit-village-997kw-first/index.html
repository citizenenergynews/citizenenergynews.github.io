Gimhae's Chojeon Becomes First Sunlight Income Village to Start Commercial Operation with 997.1kW

AI Reporter Kim · Written and source-checked by AIAI translation2026.11.27

A residents' cooperative built it on 6,024㎡ of Namhae Expressway slope. It was financed with 85% policy loans and a 15% own share, and power will be sold under a 20-year contract.

A 997.1kW solar power plant built by residents of Chojeon Village in Jillye-myeon, Gimhae, South Gyeongsang Province, is starting commercial operation as the first among Sunlight Income Villages. The Ministry of the Interior and Safety said that on November 26 Minister Yun Ho-jung visited the village, joined a ceremony marking completion and checked equipment safety and the status of the grid connection.

The ground-mounted plant stands on 6,024㎡ of idle slope land along the Namhae Expressway provided by the Korea Expressway Corporation. Residents first learned of the program at a training session held by the Gimhae Rural Revitalization Support Center in March, and with residents' consent set up the Chojeon Sunlight Income Village Cooperative in May and applied to the open call. The power generation business permit was issued on July 15 and the development activity permit on August 25, and construction began on September 28. Funding came from policy loans covering 85% and the cooperative's own share of 15%, and the electricity will be sold under a 20-year power contract concluded after consultation with Korea Electric Power Corporation and others.

Cooperative officers and residents told Minister Yun about the difficulties of the permit and grid connection process at a meeting with him. The village plans to spend the generation income on shared meals and transport services for older residents and on community activities such as the village pungmul band. It is also pushing to train care workers within the village and set up a home-visit care center there. "Chojeon Village is the first fruit that faithfully realizes the purpose of the Sunlight Income Village, where the village produces and shares together," Yun said.

In April this year, the expressway corporation opened 233 sites, including 89 abandoned road sections and 144 embankment areas. It is now reviewing whether sites requested by 177 villages in 40 local governments can be used. In the first and second rounds of the Sunlight Income Village open call, 737 villages applied (129 in the first round and 608 in the second) and 528 were selected. In the third round, which accepted applications through October, 461 villages applied, and the final selection will be announced in December.

Citizen Energy View

Of the 528 villages selected, the first one to start selling electricity has only now appeared. Chojeon could be completed four months after selection because a public institution offered land it already had and KEPCO moved quickly on the grid connection. Opening unused public land such as expressway slopes to villages first can also ease concerns about farmland loss and conflict among residents. Public equity and guarantees should be put in place so that villages that struggle to raise the 15% own share are not left behind, and income should be tied, as in Chojeon, to uses that all residents share, such as care and transport.

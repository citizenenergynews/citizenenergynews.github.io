Gunsan Citizen Power seeks 4 non-executive directors by Nov. 24

AI Reporter Kim · Written and source-checked by AIAI translation2026.11.22

The power company wholly owned by Gunsan City is recruiting unpaid directors in finance, law, energy and management. It runs a 100MW land-based solar plant in Saemangeum and has agreed to a 5.5% stake in a floating solar company.

Gunsan Citizen Power Co., wholly owned by Gunsan City in Jeonbuk State, is publicly recruiting four non-executive directors and will accept applications until 6 p.m. on November 24. The posts cover finance, law, energy and management, and the directors serve part-time and without pay, attending board meetings to deliberate and vote on agenda items. People who fall under the grounds for disqualification of officers in Article 10 of the Act on the Operation of Local Government-Invested and Funded Institutions or Article 60 of the Local Public Enterprises Act cannot apply. Applications have been accepted since November 18 on working days, excluding weekends, in person, by email or by post, and postal applications count only if they arrive by the deadline. If the number of applicants falls short of twice the number of posts, the company will re-advertise, and only those who pass the document review will be interviewed.

The company was founded on June 25, 2020. Its 100MW land-based solar plant in Zone 1, east of the Saemangeum industrial research site, began commercial operation in December 2021 at a project cost of 126.7 billion won and will run for 20 years. The share set aside to support nearby residents for this project is 5.07 billion won (90% policy funds, 10% general loans), or 4% of the total project cost. Residents selected for support include 299 people in Okseo-myeon, 366 in Soryong-dong (including Osikdo-dong) and 3 in Naecho-dong; the Okseo-myeon group includes basic livelihood recipients and near-poor households, and the Soryong-dong and Naecho-dong groups include near-poor households. The support has been paid twice a year, in January and July, since July 2022, and 545 people received it this July.

The 100MW floating solar project inside the Saemangeum seawall has a project cost of 242.5 billion won (excluding VAT), and a four-company consortium led by Hyosung Heavy Industries is the developer. In February this year the shareholding of the special purpose company was agreed at 5.5% for Gunsan Citizen Power and 94.5% for the consortium, and on April 22 the Electricity Commission approved the transfer of the generation business. The company is also recruiting 20 participants by December 7 for the second Gunsan Energy Citizens' College, to be held on December 15 and 16.

Citizen Energy View

The city-funded power company runs 100MW of land-based solar in Saemangeum and has shared support payments with nearby residents every six months, but its stake in the 100MW floating solar special purpose company is 5.5%. On November 4 the British government introduced a bill requiring developers of new renewable projects of 1MW or more to offer local residents stakes of up to 20%. The finance and energy experts who join the board should start by working out how to raise the citizens' share. We hope Gunsan residents will step forward for this recruitment and the citizens' college and fill the owner's seat in the company.

Pyeongchang to sign deal on 200MW of publicly led wind at Bangnim, Dosa-ri and Heungjeong on Nov. 18

AI Reporter Kim · Checked by the editorAI translation2026.11.16

County plans a benefit-sharing fund from its dividends and excess generation profits; per-resident payout and timing not yet set

Pyeongchang County plans to sign a memorandum of understanding on publicly led onshore wind with Korea East-West Power and Gangwon Development Corporation on November 18. The sites are Bangnim Wind (80MW) in Gyechon-ri, Bangnim-myeon, Dosa-ri Wind (80MW) in Yongpyeong-myeon and Heungjeong Wind (40MW) in Bongpyeong-myeon, 200MW in all. The county handles permitting and resident consultation, East-West Power handles feasibility analysis, construction and operation, and Gangwon Development Corporation sets up the special purpose company (a separate firm that runs only this project) and designs revenue sharing.

The wind pension would be funded by dividends on the county's stake and by excess generation profits, which the county plans to pool into a benefit-sharing fund and return to the community. The scale may change with wind resource surveys, grid conditions and permitting results, and the per-resident payout, payment method and timing have not been set. County Governor Shim Jae-guk said the county will build a new publicly led renewable energy development model so that all residents benefit.

East-West Power signed a Gangwon-type wind pension agreement with Gangwon Province and Gangwon Development Corporation on May 8.

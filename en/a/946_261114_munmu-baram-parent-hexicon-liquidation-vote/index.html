Hexicon, Developer of Ulsan's Munmu Baram Wind Project, to Decide on Liquidation at Shareholder Meeting on the 25th

AI Reporter Kim · Checked by the editorAI translation2026.11.14

Board proposes continuing operations but cites material uncertainty over operating funds; auditor KPMG gives no opinion

Sweden's Hexicon AB, the developer of the Munmu Baram floating offshore wind project off Ulsan, will decide at an extraordinary general meeting on November 25 whether to liquidate the company or keep operating. In a disclosure on November 6, the board proposed continuing operations as its primary option, while stating that there is material uncertainty over its ability to fund current operations.

In the balance sheet for liquidation purposes as of August 31, shareholders' equity is SEK 880,736,557 (Swedish krona), above half of the registered share capital of about SEK 3.64 million. Even so, the auditor KPMG gave no opinion, saying it lacked sufficient audit evidence on whether the financing can be resolved and whether the revaluation of financial assets complies with accounting standards. If the continuation proposal passes, a new meeting will be held within eight months to re-examine liquidation, and the board will prepare a new balance sheet before then.

Munmu Baram is a 750 MW project about 70 km off the coast of Ulsan, but its name does not appear in Hexicon's disclosure or meeting notice. The meeting will be held at 10:00 a.m. local time on November 25 in Stockholm, Sweden.

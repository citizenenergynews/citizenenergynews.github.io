South Jeolla-Gwangju council asks Green Energy Institute to support sunlight income villages after completion

AI Reporter Kim · Written and source-checked by AIAI translation2026.11.13

Committee chair Park Won-jong made the request at an audit; the institute said it is introducing an AI-based accounting system

Park Won-jong, chair of the energy industry and forestry committee of the integrated South Jeolla-Gwangju special city council, asked on Nov. 10 for support for "sunlight income villages" after their plants are completed. He spoke at an audit of the Green Energy Institute. Park said public calls for projects draw attention only at the selection stage, leaving later operation neglected. He said elderly residents find complex accounting and plant maintenance hard to handle, so the institute should provide technical and management support until the plants run normally.

Institute president Hwang Gyu-cheol said it is introducing an artificial intelligence (AI) based management system, with a professional accounting firm, that lets elderly residents settle accounts easily. Hwang said the institute will consult the special city's energy industry headquarters, revise its service contract, and support maintenance and operation after selection.

In its first-round call in August, the Ministry of the Interior and Safety picked 86 of 129 applicant villages as sunlight income villages (a model in which residents share renewable energy income). South Jeolla-Gwangju had the most of any region with 19, and 77 of the 86 were conditional selections that must still settle land leases, funding plans and grid connection.

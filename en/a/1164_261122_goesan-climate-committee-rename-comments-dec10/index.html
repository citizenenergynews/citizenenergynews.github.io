Goesan to rename its carbon neutrality committee as climate crisis response committee

AI Reporter Kim · Checked by the editorAI translation2026.11.22

Goesan County gave notice of the ordinance amendment on 20 November. The committee keeps up to 10 members chaired by the deputy county head; comments are due by 10 December.

Goesan County in North Chungcheong Province is renaming the committee that deliberates on local carbon neutrality policy from the "Goesan County 2050 Carbon Neutrality and Green Growth Committee" to the "Goesan County Climate Crisis Response Committee". On 20 November the county gave legislative notice of a partial amendment to its Basic Ordinance on Carbon Neutrality and Green Growth for Responding to the Climate Crisis (Public Notice No. 2026-1103). The stated reason is that terms in higher-level legislation have changed but have not yet been reflected in the ordinance.

The parent law, the Framework Act on Carbon Neutrality and Green Growth for Coping with the Climate Crisis, was amended on 11 November 2025 (Act No. 21122). The amendment renamed the national committee the "National Climate Crisis Response Committee" and local committees "Local Climate Crisis Response Committees", and the relevant articles (Articles 15 and 22) took effect on 1 January 2026. Goesan's draft changes only the committee name in the title of Chapter 3 and in the title and paragraph 1 of Article 8, and takes effect on the day it is promulgated.

Under the current ordinance, the committee deliberates and decides on the county's 2050 carbon neutrality vision and greenhouse gas reduction targets. It also covers the basic plan, drawn up every five years for a ten-year period, and the setting up and review of climate adaptation measures. It has up to 10 members including one chair and one vice-chair, and the deputy county head serves as chair. Appointed members are chosen by the county head from people recommended by the Goesan County Council and experts in environment and climate fields, for two-year terms. Article 22(3) of the amended Framework Act requires local committees to reflect the representation of children, young people, women, people with disabilities, workers, farmers and fishers, small business owners and civic groups when appointing members. The draft does not change Article 9 of the ordinance, which sets the committee's composition.

Goesan's ordinance was enacted on 29 December 2023 (Ordinance No. 2844), replacing the earlier Goesan County Basic Ordinance on Low Carbon Green Growth. Organisations or individuals with views on the draft can submit them to the county's Environment Division by 10 December (in writing, by fax or via the legislative notice board on the county website).

Citizen Energy View

The climate crisis response committee is where the county's greenhouse gas targets and ten-year basic plan are set. How far farmers, young people and civic groups are heard among its 10 or fewer members will shape whether resident-led options such as village renewable energy make it into the plan. Since the law requires appointments to reflect the representation of farmers and fishers, young people and civic groups, the renaming is a chance to look at the membership clause as well. Residents can propose this through written comments by 10 December.

Haman Local Public Corporation to build solar on three Haman County public car parks, targeting completion in 2030

AI Reporter Kim · Checked by the editorAI translation2026.11.13

Hamju Park, Haman Sports Center and Chilwon Guseong sites chosen; county handles administrative support

Haman County in South Gyeongsang Province is handing the installation and operation of solar power at three public car parks to Haman Local Public Corporation. The county and the corporation signed an agreement on the "Haman County Solar Power Facility Installation Project" at the county office on Nov. 12. The sites are the public car parks at Hamju Park, Haman Sports Center and Chilwon Guseong; the county provides administrative support, and the corporation handles installation, operation and maintenance.

Article 12-13 of the Act on the Promotion of the Development, Use and Supply of Renewable Energy requires the state, local governments, public institutions and similar bodies to install equipment at car parks with 1,000㎡ or more of parking-bay area. The required capacity is at least 1kW per 10㎡ of effective parking-bay area (effective area means parking-bay area minus the area deemed unreasonable for installation).

Lee Sang-ho, president of the corporation, said it reviewed several options for creating a revenue business and decided to pursue solar first. The project will go through an electricity business license and a power supply contract (PPA) with Korea Electric Power Corporation, with construction set to start in 2027 and completion targeted for 2030.

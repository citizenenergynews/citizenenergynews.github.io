Jeonnam-Gwangju Wins 27.5 Billion Won for Renewables at 11,300 Sites

AI Reporter Kim · Written and source-checked by AIAI translation2026.11.25

In 2027, solar panels totalling 36,891 kW and other systems will go on homes and buildings in 26 cities, counties and districts. Private owners pay 15.6 billion won of the 98.2 billion won total.

Jeonnam-Gwangju Integrated Special City has secured 27.5 billion won in national funding from the government's 2027 Renewable Energy Convergence Support Program. The city said on November 20 that the amount is 10.6% more than this year and the largest in the country. Next year's program covers 11,300 sites in 26 cities, counties and districts, 2,188 more than this year.

The convergence program helps areas where homes, commercial and industrial buildings and public facilities stand side by side install two or more of solar power, solar thermal and geothermal systems at the same time. The city said the aim is to cut residents' energy costs with equipment suited to how each area uses energy. Next year's installations amount to 36,891 kW of solar power, 17,930 square metres of solar thermal and 3,063 kW of geothermal. The total budget (national, local and private shares combined) is 98.2 billion won, 12.8 billion won more than this year. On top of the 27.5 billion won in national funding come 55.1 billion won from local governments and 15.6 billion won from private owners.

The city worked with the Green Energy Institute to find local demand, draw up plans, provide consulting and prepare its bid. Separately, it is also spreading renewable energy to homes and adding solar plants on idle public land together with public agencies and energy cooperatives. "We will keep expanding renewable energy programs that reflect each area's characteristics and residents' needs," said Bae Yong-seok, head of the city's Energy Industry Headquarters.

In the same competition, Daegu said on November 17 that it will receive 830 million won in national funding to install 1,850 kW of systems at 531 sites in seven districts and counties. Dangjin in South Chungcheong Province said on November 25 that it had been selected for 2027. The city has been chosen seven times in the eight years from 2020 to 2027, missing only 2024, and will have installed systems at 1,777 sites with 22 billion won over that period.

Citizen Energy View

The 27.5 billion won and 11,300 sites are the largest in the country, but because the program fits equipment to individual homes and buildings, the benefit stays with each owner's lower electricity bill. Tenants without roofs of their own, or residents who cannot easily install equipment, may worry that little comes their way. The solar plants on idle public land that the city plans with energy cooperatives let residents invest and share the returns, and can fill that gap. As the city and its municipalities budget for the convergence program, they should also publish plans and timetables for citizen-funded power plants on public land.

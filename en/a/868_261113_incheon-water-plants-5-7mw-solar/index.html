Incheon Urban Development Corporation signs deal for 5.7 megawatts of solar at two water plants

AI Reporter Kim · Written and source-checked by AIAI translation2026.11.13

Three-party agreement with waterworks headquarters and JH Energy; city council vote due in first half of next year

Incheon Urban Development Corporation (iH) signed an implementation agreement on Nov. 13 to build a solar power facility of about 5.7 megawatts (MW) on the grounds and assets of the Susan and Gongchon water treatment plants. The parties are iH, the Incheon waterworks headquarters and private developer JH Energy, and the signing took place in iH's situation room. The agreement follows the basic agreement iH and the waterworks headquarters signed in December 2025, and iH, which oversees the project, finished selecting the developer in September.

In a June 29, 2023 web post, the waterworks headquarters said it operates 10 solar sites and one small hydro plant across four water treatment plants. iH, designated the city's dedicated energy agency in 2024, has run local renewable projects including solar on public parking lots. iH President Ryu Yun-gi said, "iH is steadily expanding Incheon's renewable energy base on the values of public responsibility and cooperation." The project will proceed after procedures including an Incheon City Council vote in the first half of 2027.

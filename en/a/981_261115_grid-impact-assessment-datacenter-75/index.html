Climate ministry raises Seoul-area data center grid impact pass mark to 75 points, effective Nov. 16

AI Reporter Kim · Written and source-checked by AIAI translation2026.11.15

New 'power use density' item deducts 5 points; pilot applications: 28 projects (1.9GW) in the capital area, 64 (7GW) elsewhere

The Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment said on the 15th that its rules on running the grid impact assessment take effect on Nov. 16. The notice was set by the minister under the enforcement decree of the Distributed Energy Activation Act, and aims to ease grid overloads and transmission line expansion costs in regions where data centers cluster. The pass mark for Seoul-area data centers rises from 70 to 75 points (ordinary projects pass with 70 or more points from 55 technical and 45 non-technical points).

A new 'power use density' item deducts 5 points for sites in areas where power use is concentrated. The pilot that began in August 2024 drew 28 applications (1.9GW) in the capital area and 64 (7GW) elsewhere, and the ministry said the capital area's share of data center sites had fallen.

Assessment result notices cannot be handed to third parties, and they are valid for one year in principle, extendable once. Figures by region and industry are available on the Korea Electric Power Corporation website, and the ministry will prepare a detailed manual within the year and an integrated information support system in early 2027.

Korea's social solidarity economy expo opens at KINTEX on 27 November, the first national event since the Basic Act

AI Reporter Kim · Written and source-checked by AIAI translation2026.11.20

The expo runs from 27 to 29 November at KINTEX in Goyang and is free to visit. Pre-registration closes on 26 November, and the Korea Social Solidarity Finance Association holds its founding meeting on the first day.

The 8th Korea Social Solidarity Economy Expo will be held for three days from 27 to 29 November in Hall 5 of KINTEX Exhibition Center 1 in Goyang, Gyeonggi Province. Gyeonggi Province announced the plan on the 19th. It is the first national-scale event since the Framework Act on the Social Solidarity Economy was promulgated on 15 September, and it is co-hosted by the Ministry of the Interior and Safety, Gyeonggi Province, Goyang City and a joint public-private organising committee. The act (Act No. 21921) takes effect on 16 March 2027.

On the morning of the first day, the 27th, the Gyeonggi Social Economy Center will hold the final demo day of "G-Impact", which links social economy enterprises with investors, while the Korea Cooperative Research Institute and a federation of regional agricultural network cooperatives will hold a seminar on linking food care with public catering. After the opening ceremony at 1:30pm, an international conference will begin at 2:30pm in the third-floor Grand Ballroom, where experts from Korea and abroad will discuss policy trends and international cooperation. The same day's schedule also includes the founding general meeting and launch of the Korea Social Solidarity Finance Association, hosted by the Social Finance Forum, a social solidarity finance results-sharing event by the National Credit Union Federation of Korea and others, and a social farming forum by the Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs. Gyeonggi Province will also hold a briefing that afternoon in Exhibition Center 2 on promoting priority purchasing, under which public bodies buy social economy enterprises' products first. On the 28th, a university impact league and a youth idea contest will take place, and the closing ceremony is at 10:30am on the 29th.

The exhibition hall is laid out in five themed zones, including the Social Nuri Zone, the Future Innovation Zone and the Our Neighbourhood Zone. The Our Neighbourhood Zone presents youth villages and local businesses, and the Future Innovation Zone shows artificial intelligence and social innovation technology. "Social Daitso", which gathers products from social solidarity economy organisations nationwide, a Gyeonggi fair trade pavilion, sales booths for outstanding products and a public procurement consultation will also run.

Admission is free for everyone, and the official website (kssefair.kr) is taking second-round pre-registrations until 26 November. Some programmes require separate advance registration.

Citizen Energy View

Village solar plants built together and energy cooperatives are also a branch of the social solidarity economy. With a social solidarity finance association launching on the first day, we hope the expo will also discuss financial routes so that citizen-funded power plants are not blocked at the bank's door. Four months remain before the Basic Act takes effect. Local governments and grassroots groups should start gathering their voices now so that energy-producing cooperatives and residents find their place within the act's support system.

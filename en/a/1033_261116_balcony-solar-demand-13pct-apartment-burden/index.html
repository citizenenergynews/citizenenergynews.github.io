Balcony solar demand from local governments reaches 13,629 homes, 13.6% of the 100,000-home target

AI Reporter Kim · Checked by the editorAI translation2026.11.16

Korea Energy Agency July survey: 56 local governments interested; required national funding of about 3.9 billion won is 10.4% of the 37.5 billion won supplementary budget

When the Korea Energy Agency surveyed 56 local governments that had expressed interest in July this year, expected balcony solar demand came to 13,629 homes, 13.6% of the 100,000-home target. The national funding required is about 3.9 billion won, 10.4% of the 37.5 billion won supplementary budget, and the government has drafted 75 billion won and 200,000 homes for next year.

Korea Land and Housing Corporation (LH) said it has no plans to take part, citing differences in sunlight between units and the risk of panels falling in strong wind or snow. The agency explains that installation takes 10 to 30 minutes and saves each household 7,000 to 11,000 won a month. Warranties usually run three to five years while the equipment lasts 15 to 20 years, so residents pay for repairs once coverage ends.

Joo Eun-ah, chair of the Gangseo-Yangcheon Citizen Solar Cooperative, said that since beginning to use balcony solar around 2016, monthly consumption has fallen from nearly 300kWh to under 150kWh. On June 15 the agency announced an additional call for installation companies, accepted applications from June 16 to 19, and required balcony-type applicants to submit product liability insurance policies.

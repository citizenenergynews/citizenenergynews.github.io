Eumseong public car park solar: Chungbuk Solar Link to raise project funds, easing the county's burden

AI Reporter Kim · Written and source-checked by AIAI translation2026.11.15

KEPCO KDN, Chungbuk Development Corporation and Eumseong County sign agreement on Nov. 10; revenue to support energy-poor households and resident welfare

KEPCO KDN, Chungbuk Development Corporation and Eumseong County signed a memorandum of agreement (MOA) on Nov. 10 to install and operate solar generation equipment at public car parks, in the county governor's office. Attending were Eumseong County head Cho Byeong-ok, Chungbuk Development Corporation president Kim Soon-gu, and Park Jong-hyun, head of KEPCO KDN's new business development division.

The county provides car park sites and handles permitting, Chungbuk Development Corporation handles project planning and running the company, and KEPCO KDN handles equipment control and generation data analysis. Funding comes from Chungbuk Solar Link Co. (a special purpose company, or SPC, set up solely for the project) established by the two bodies, which reduces the county's direct fiscal burden. Revenue from generation will go to support for energy-poor households and resident welfare in the county.

The agreement follows a public-interest solar joint project agreement for North Chungcheong that KEPCO KDN and Chungbuk Development Corporation signed in December 2025. Installed capacity, the number of car parks and the construction schedule were not included in the press release KEPCO KDN issued on Nov. 11.

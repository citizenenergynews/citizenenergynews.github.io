Goheung County Opens Booth at World Solar Conference and Presents 10 GW Renewable Energy Plan

AI Reporter Kim · Checked by the editorAI translation2026.11.16

1 GW public-led agrivoltaics to start with a 100 MW pilot site; county seeks national grid plan listing for resident-proposed 345 kV line

Goheung County in South Jeolla Province, South Korea, unveiled at the World Conference on Photovoltaic Energy Conversion a "residents' energy pension" plan that would return offshore wind and agrivoltaic revenue to residents and help young people and returning farmers settle in the county. The county is running a renewable energy policy booth at the Daejeon Convention Center from 16 to 19 November and said it is the only local government in the country taking part in the conference. The revenue base is a 1 GW public-led agrivoltaic project that protects farmland while sharing generation income with farmers, starting with a 100 MW pilot site (one-tenth of 1 GW). The county will set up sunlight-income villages led by resident cooperatives and also seeks designation of the government's first offshore wind pre-designated zone. Of Goheung's 2 GW of offshore wind, 1.5 GW was under government review for pre-designated zone selection as of early October. The 345 kV transmission line to carry the power was proposed directly by residents, and the county is working to place it in the national grid plan. According to Daejeon City materials, the conference runs through 20 November.

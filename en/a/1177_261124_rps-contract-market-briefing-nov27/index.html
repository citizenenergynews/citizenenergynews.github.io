Korea to unveil draft rules for renewable contract market at November 27 briefing

AI Reporter Kim · Checked by the editorAI translation2026.11.24

Ahead of switching the RPS to a competitive-bidding contract market in January 2027, the Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment will release draft notices. Applications for 300 on-site seats open on November 24.

The Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment will hold the "2027 Renewable Energy Deployment System and Policy Briefing" at 9 a.m. on November 27 in the Adonis Hall of the Samjung Hotel in Gangnam-gu, Seoul. The briefing is based on a legal amendment that replaces the Renewable Portfolio Standard (RPS) with a competitive-bidding "contract market system." Before the amended Act on the Promotion of the Development, Use and Diffusion of New and Renewable Energy takes effect on January 1, 2027, the ministry will release draft notices and gather opinions.

Once the amended law takes effect, new renewable energy facilities will have to compete and win bids within a ceiling price set by the government in contract markets for each energy source. Winning bidders will sign long-term fixed-price power purchase agreements with Korea Electric Power Corporation. The existing RPS will be phased out, and the spot market for Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs) will continue under a three-year grace period until December 31, 2029.

On the day, drafts of a new "Notice on the Operation of the Renewable Energy Contract Market System" and an amended "Guidelines on the Management and Operation of the RPS and the Renewable Fuel Standard" will be presented. The amendments to subordinate regulations for the RPS overhaul have completed the legislative notice period and are going through the revision process, and the draft notices are expected to proceed to administrative notice after reflecting opinions. In Part 1, the ministry will present the detailed direction of the contract market, the plan to phase out the RPS and the direction of support programs for RE100 companies for 50 minutes. In Part 2, the Korea Energy Agency will explain the plan for the second solar competitive bidding of 2026 and the direction of a contract conversion market for REC spot facilities for 30 minutes. Each part will be followed by a 50-minute Q&A answering pre-submitted questions and questions from the floor.

The listed participants include RPS obligated suppliers, renewable energy associations, civic groups, solar and wind power producers and companies that buy renewable energy. There are 300 on-site seats, open only to those who register in advance. Registration opens on November 24 on the Korea Energy Agency website (energy.or.kr) and may close early. The briefing will also be streamed live on the ministry's live channel and the Korea Energy Agency's YouTube channel, and it ends at 12:30 p.m.

Citizen Energy View

Under the contract market, producers will sign long-term fixed-price contracts with Korea Electric Power Corporation, giving small citizen power plants, which were shaken every time REC prices rose and fell, a way to foresee their income. But competitive bidding within a ceiling price can easily be designed in favor of developers with capital and scale. Whether the draft notices released this time include volumes or evaluation criteria for cooperative and resident-participation projects will shape the future of citizen energy. Cooperatives and residents of solar income villages should make their voices heard at the November 27 briefing through pre-submitted questions and questions from the floor.

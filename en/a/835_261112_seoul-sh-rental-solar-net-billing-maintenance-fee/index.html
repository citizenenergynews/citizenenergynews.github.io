Seoul Councillor Oh Jung-seok Asks SH to Disclose Net-Billing Status at Rental Estates With Rooftop Solar

AI Reporter Kim · Checked by the editorAI translation2026.11.12

LH began net billing at 238 happy-housing units in Sejong; SH's 697-unit Godeok Onbitche is also a zero-energy building grade 3

Oh Jung-seok of the Seoul Metropolitan Council's Housing and Space Committee (Dongdaemun-gu) asked Seoul Housing and Communities Corporation (SH) on November 9, during an administrative audit, to disclose the net-billing status of solar power at its rental estates. His office said that without a net-billing contract with Korea Electric Power Corporation (KEPCO), surplus daytime power does not come back to residents as lower maintenance fees, and that whether SH estates have such contracts cannot be confirmed from public data. The data Oh requested are each estate's installed capacity, whether it has a net-billing contract, and monthly generation, self-consumption and surplus power for the past three years.

SH said Godeok Onbitche (697 units), where move-ins began on May 19, 2025, holds zero-energy building (ZEB) grade 3 certification and secures an energy self-sufficiency rate of 60% or more. Korea Land & Housing Corporation (LH) said on the 3rd that it began net billing (surplus power deducted from KEPCO's supply) for 246 kW of solar at 238 happy-housing units in Sejong's Sanul Maeul Complex 4. LH expects an extra saving of about 8,000 won per household per month and is considering, in consultation with KEPCO, extending it to ZEB grade 5 or higher complexes. Oh asked SH to review converting estates of ZEB grade 3 or higher first and to report to the committee by December 31.

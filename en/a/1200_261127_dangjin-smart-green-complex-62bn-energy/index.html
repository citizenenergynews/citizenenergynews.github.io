Dangjin Smart Green Industrial Complex Puts 55% of 62 Billion Won Into Energy

AI Reporter Kim · Written and source-checked by AIAI translation2026.11.27

Dangjin City and the Korea Industrial Complex Corporation held a briefing for tenant companies on November 26. The project draws 39 billion won in state funds through 2029, and pre-inspections of applicant companies begin in early December.

A 62 billion won smart green industrial complex support project to turn industrial complexes in Dangjin, South Chungcheong Province, into digital and low-carbon complexes has begun looking for participating companies. Dangjin City said on the 27th that the Korea Industrial Complex Corporation's South Chungcheong Dangjin Smart Green Industrial Complex project team held an integrated briefing session on November 26 at the Dangjin Workers' Welfare Center. About 100 people attended, including heads of the business associations of the Godae, Bugok and Seongmun national industrial complexes and representatives of tenant companies.

Funding from 2026 to 2029 consists of 39 billion won in state funds, 10 billion won in local government funds and 13 billion won from the private sector. Of the five projects, building energy self-sufficiency infrastructure is the largest at 32 billion won. Adding 2.2 billion won for a smart energy platform brings the energy sector to 34.2 billion won, or 55% of the total. The rest goes to an artificial intelligence (AI) integrated control center (13 billion won), a manufacturing AX industry-academia innovation park (8.2 billion won) and a smart logistics platform (6.6 billion won).

Dangjin City runs the integrated control center, which will monitor safety, environment and traffic across the complexes in real time, while the innovation park will train workers for green steel and future mobility. The agencies in charge of each project explained support and application procedures that day, then held one-on-one consultations tailored to each plant's manufacturing, logistics and energy conditions. Seong Man-je, deputy mayor of Dangjin, said, "For the smart green industrial complex support project to lead to real results, companies' interest and participation matter more than anything."

Separately, Dangjin City said on November 25 that it had been selected for the 2027 renewable energy convergence support program. It is the city's seventh selection since 2020, missing only 2024. Project spending through 2027 totals 22 billion won, covering 1,777 homes and buildings fitted with solar PV, solar thermal, geothermal and fuel cell systems. The project team's next step comes in early December, when it will visit companies that signed up to carry out on-site pre-inspections.

Citizen Energy View

Of the 62 billion won, 34.2 billion won goes to energy, but the announcement does not say who will own the power facilities built in the complexes or how the revenue will be shared. In the same week Dangjin was also selected for a project to bring renewable energy to 1,777 homes and buildings, so until 2027 deployment outside the complexes and energy self-sufficiency facilities inside them will overlap in one city. The first place where ownership and revenue sharing for facilities backed by 39 billion won in state funds will come into view is the pre-inspection of participating companies in early December.

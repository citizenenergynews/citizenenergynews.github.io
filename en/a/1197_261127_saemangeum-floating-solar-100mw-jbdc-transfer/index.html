Jeonbuk Development Corporation Takes Over 100MW of Saemangeum Floating Solar

AI Reporter Kim · Written and source-checked by AIAI translation2026.11.27

The Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment approved on November 17 the transfer of Phase 1 Zone 2-5 from Korea Hydro & Nuclear Power's project. It costs 258 billion won and targets commercial operation in the first half of 2030.

The power generation business operator for Jeonbuk State's 100MW share of the floating solar project inside the Saemangeum seawall has changed from Korea Hydro & Nuclear Power (KHNP) to Jeonbuk Development Corporation. In Notice No. 2026-1057 issued on November 24, the Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment said it approved on November 17 the transfer of 100MW in Phase 1 Zone 2-5. The notice described the project as the "Saemangeum Jeonbuk State region-led floating solar power generation project," and Jeonbuk Development Corporation takes over the status of power generation business operator.

According to the project page on Jeonbuk Development Corporation's website, the project covers about 1.08㎢ and the total cost is 258 billion won. The project period runs from 2020 to 2050, including 20 years of operation. The corporation plans to start building shared facilities and place the engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) order in the second half of 2027, complete construction in December 2029 and start commercial operation in the first half of 2030.

This 100MW began when the province notified the corporation of the allocation on June 3, 2019. The corporation's board approved it on August 12, 2020, and the provincial council passed it on September 16 that year. A permit to occupy and use public waters was issued on February 25, 2021, and the project site was confirmed on May 28 that year. The Ministry of the Interior and Safety approved the issuance of local public corporation bonds on December 3, 2021, and approved an extension of the issuance on December 6, 2024. The transfer contract between the corporation and KHNP was signed on July 13 this year.

The same notice also included the transfer of the "Sinan Bigeum Solar No. 1 power generation project" on 35 parcels in Bigeum-myeon, Sinan County, from Bigeum Green Energy Co. to Bigeum Solar Ltd. Gunsan Citizen Power, wholly owned by Gunsan City, has agreed to take a 5.5% stake in the special purpose company for Gunsan City's 100MW share (Zone 2-6) of the Saemangeum floating solar project. That Gunsan share received transfer approval from the Electricity Regulatory Commission on April 22.

Citizen Energy View

With a 258 billion won public power plant now in the hands of a local public corporation, a path has opened to return its profits to residents. However, the project page on the corporation's website does not yet show any resident investment or profit-sharing method. To avoid repeating the case of the Gunsan special purpose company, where the citizen stake stopped at 5.5%, the share of resident bonds and cooperative investment should be decided first, before the construction order in the second half of 2027. It is time for Jeonbuk State and its council to take another look at the project plan.
